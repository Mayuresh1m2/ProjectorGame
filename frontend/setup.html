2 · Test pattern

Drag this browser window to the projector, then show a full-screen grid with margins, a circle, colour bars, a grey ramp and text sizes. Use it to check coverage, focus, brightness and keystone.

Press Esc or click anywhere to leave. In the pattern, the outer border is the full picture, the inner dashed box is the 90% safe area, and the circle should look round (if it looks like an oval, fix the projector angle or aspect ratio).

3 · Latency test

Measures how long it takes from the projector showing a flash to the laptop receiving the camera frame that shows it. This includes projector delay, phone capture, Wi-Fi and decoding.

  1. Start the iPhone camera (Control panel → Camera).
  2. Aim the phone at the projected picture, or at a patch of wall it lights. Dim the room.
  3. Move this window to the projector, then press the button.
